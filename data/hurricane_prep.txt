Hurricane Prep Quick Guide
==========================
Scope: Tropical storms and hurricanes.

Before Landfall
- Pre-stage generators, fuel, and mobile communications.
- Confirm hospital and long-term care facility plans.
- Test satellite phones; verify mutual aid agreements.

During Impact
- Maintain situational awareness: wind speed, storm surge, rainfall.
- Close drawbridges; curfew if debris and power outages escalate.
- Coordinate debris clearance priorities with public works.

After Impact
- Search & rescue sweeps; safety inspections for reentry.
- Damage assessment teams; initial claims support booths.
- Public health messaging: carbon monoxide, chainsaw safety.

Citations/References
- Local hurricane annex and surge zone maps.
