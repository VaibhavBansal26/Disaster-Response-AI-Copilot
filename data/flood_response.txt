Flood Response Checklist
=========================
Scope: Urban and riverine flooding incidents.

Immediate Actions (Golden 60 Minutes)
1. Confirm life safety: call-trees, vulnerable populations, special needs.
2. Avoid driving through flooded roads; set barricades at underpasses and low-water crossings.
3. Establish incident command; assign public information officer (PIO).
4. Monitor river gauges and rainfall radar; pre-stage sandbags and pumps.
5. Route EMS access; coordinate with utilities for power isolation.

Shelter & Evacuation
- Voluntary evacuation for Zone A when river rises above minor flood stage.
- Open shelters above 100-year floodplain; verify ADA access, pets policy.
- Transportation for seniors and mobility-limited residents.

Public Health
- Issue boil-water notices if treatment plants compromised.
- Watch for waterborne illness; distribute cleaning kits and bleach guidance.

Citations/References
- Local floodplain management plan, evacuation zones map, and emergency operations plan (EOP).
