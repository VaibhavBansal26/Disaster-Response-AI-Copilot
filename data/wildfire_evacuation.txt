Wildfire Evacuation Guide
=========================
Scope: Wildland-urban interface (WUI) incidents.

Immediate Actions
1. Issue evacuation orders by zones (A/B/C) with clear routes.
2. Coordinate traffic control at chokepoints; contraflow if needed.
3. Set structure triage: defendable vs. non-defendable.
4. Air quality monitoring (AQI); distribute N95s; open clean-air shelters.
5. Utilities: consider public safety power shutoffs (PSPS).

Operational Considerations
- Night operations staffing; lookout communications; wind shift briefings.
- Spot fire detection and rapid initial attack resources.
- Public messaging templates (social, SMS, IPAWS).

Citations/References
- Local CWPP (Community Wildfire Protection Plan) and evacuation zone map.
